## 1. Kahneman & Deaton (2010) PNAS — 情緒幸福感的「高原」與生活評價的持續上升

- **主題**：年收入與情緒幸福感（emotional well-being）／生活評價（life evaluation）的關係
- **事實**：情緒幸福感隨 log(收入) 上升，但在年收入約 **7.5 萬美元**（~$75,000）之後不再進一步上升；生活評價則隨 log(收入) 持續上升，沒有觀察到平頂。
- **數字**：~$75,000/年（美國全國樣本，2008–2009 年 Gallup-Healthways 資料，450,000+ 筆回答）
- **來源機構**：Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS)
- **網址**：https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2944762（PMC 全文）；DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1011492107
- **以…為準**：PMC2944762 全文摘要（已直接打開讀取，逐字抄錄）
- **信心等級**：verified
- **原文引述**（摘要全文，逐字）：
  > "When plotted against log income, life evaluation rises steadily. Emotional well-being also rises with log income, but there is no further progress beyond an annual income of ~$75,000. ... We conclude that high income buys life satisfaction but not happiness, and that low income is associated both with low life evaluation and low emotional well-being."
- **備註**：完整引用：Kahneman, D., & Deaton, A. (2010). High income improves evaluation of life but not emotional well-being. *PNAS*, 107(38), 16489–16493. 情緒幸福感測量方式是問「昨天」的情緒經驗（二分法選項），生活評價用 Cantril's Self-Anchoring Scale（0–10 階梯量表）。

## 2. Killingsworth (2021) PNAS — 「經驗幸福感在 7.5 萬美元以上仍持續上升」

- **主題**：用手機即時取樣（experience sampling）重新檢驗 7.5 萬美元高原是否存在
- **事實**：以 1,725,994 筆即時回報（33,391 名美國在職成人）分析，經驗幸福感與生活評價都隨 log(收入) 呈線性上升，**沒有觀察到 7.5 萬美元的平頂**；收入 8 萬美元以下與以上的斜率幾乎相同（b = 0.109 vs. b = 0.110，皆 P < 0.00001）。
- **數字**：樣本中位數家戶年收入 $85,000（25th percentile $45,000；75th percentile $137,500）
- **來源機構**：PNAS
- **網址**：https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2016976118（DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2016976118）；全文 PDF 直接讀取自 https://wpa.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Experienced-Well-Being-Rises-with-Income-January-2021.pdf
- **以…為準**：本人直接下載並逐頁讀取全文 PDF（非摘要轉述）
- **信心等級**：verified
- **原文引述**（Abstract，逐字）：
  > "Drawing on 1,725,994 experience-sampling reports from 33,391 employed US adults, the present results show that both experienced and evaluative well-being increased linearly with log(income), with an equally steep slope for higher earners as for lower earners. There was no evidence for an experienced well-being plateau above $75,000/y, contrary to some influential past research."
- **原文引述**（正文，關於斜率比較）：
  > "the slope of the association between log(income) and experienced well-being was virtually identical for incomes below and up to $80,000/y (b = 0.109, P < 0.00001) as it was for incomes larger than $80,000/y (b = 0.110, P < 0.00001)."
- **備註**：Killingsworth, M. A. (2021). Experienced well-being rises with income, even above $75,000 per year. *PNAS*, 118(4), e2016976118. 此文明確把自己定位為對 Kahneman & Deaton (2010) 及 Jebb et al. (2018) 的方法學挑戰（後兩者用二分法量表，天花板效應嚴重）。

## 3. Killingsworth, Kahneman & Mellers (2023) PNAS — 「對抗合作」：高原只在最不快樂的一群人身上

- **主題**：Kahneman（主張有高原）與 Killingsworth（主張沒有高原）的對抗式合作（adversarial collaboration），第三方仲裁者 Barbara Mellers
- **事實**：重新分析 Killingsworth 的即時取樣資料後發現：**高原型態只出現在最不快樂的一群人身上**；在較快樂的人身上，幸福感隨 log(收入) 持續穩定上升，甚至在最快樂的一群人中還會加速上升。
- **數字**：Significance 摘要明確寫「the least happy **20%**」；Kahneman & Deaton 原本發現的平頂區間，本文重新描述為「somewhere between **$60,000 and $90,000**」（而非單一的 $75,000 這個點）。
- **來源機構**：PNAS
- **網址**：https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2208661120（DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2208661120）；全文經 PMC10013834 讀取
- **以…為準**：PMC10013834 全文的 Abstract 與 Significance 兩段（已直接打開，逐字抄錄，非轉述）
- **信心等級**：verified
- **原文引述**（Significance box，逐字）：
  > "Kahneman and Deaton reported an exception; a measure of emotional well-being (happiness) increased but then flattened somewhere between $60,000 and $90,000. In contrast, Killingsworth observed a linear relation between happiness and log(income) in an experience-sampling study. We discovered in a joint reanalysis of the experience sampling data that the flattening pattern exists but is restricted to the least happy 20% of the population, and that complementary nonlinearities contribute to the overall linear-log relationship between happiness and income."
- **原文引述**（Abstract，逐字）：
  > "A reanalysis of Killingsworth's experienced sampling data confirmed the flattening pattern only for the least happy people. Happiness increases steadily with log(income) among happier people, and even accelerates in the happiest group."
- **備註**：完整引用：Killingsworth, M. A., Kahneman, D., & Mellers, B. (2023). Income and emotional well-being: A conflict resolved. *PNAS*, 120(10), e2208661120. 網路上有部分二手報導把平頂數字寫成「$100,000」（inflation-adjusted 版本，出現在論文正文而非 Significance box），與 Significance box 的「$60,000–$90,000」不完全一致，兩者都是原文出現過的數字，但指涉的段落不同（正文 vs. 摘要），使用時建議標明是哪一段的說法，避免混用。此文後續在 PNAS 上有兩篇評論性回應文章對其因果假設提出質疑（Inappropriate causal assumptions...；The miseries that remain...），屬於學術界的後續爭論，非本次查證範圍核心，僅供背景參考。

## 4. Van Boven & Gilovich (2003) JPSP — 「體驗 vs. 物質」

- **主題**：體驗性消費（experiential purchases）是否比物質性消費（material purchases）帶來更多快樂
- **事實**：兩份調查顯示，受訪者認為以獲得生活體驗為主要目的的消費，比物質性消費更讓他們快樂；後續實驗室實驗也顯示，回想體驗性消費比回想物質性消費帶來更多正向情緒。
- **來源機構**：Journal of Personality and Social Psychology (JPSP)
- **網址**：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14674824/；DOI: 10.1037/0022-3514.85.6.1193
- **以…為準**：WebFetch 直接取得的摘要全文（逐字）
- **信心等級**：verified
- **原文引述**（Abstract，逐字）：
  > "Do experiences make people happier than material possessions? In two surveys, respondents from various demographic groups indicated that experiential purchases—those made with the primary intention of acquiring a life experience—made them happier than material purchases. In a follow-up laboratory experiment, participants experienced more positive feelings after pondering an experiential purchase than after pondering a material purchase... The discussion focuses on evidence that experiences make people happier because they are more open to positive reinterpretations, are a more meaningful part of one's identity, and contribute more to successful social relationships."
- **完整引用**：Van Boven, L., & Gilovich, T. (2003). To do or to have? That is the question. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 85(6), 1193–1202.

### 4a. 後續修正／邊界條件：Weidman & Dunn (2016)

- **事實**：這篇後續研究比較「當下」（momentary）的快樂感受，發現**物質性購買帶來的快樂比較「頻繁」，體驗性購買帶來的快樂比較「強烈」但較不頻繁**——兩者互有優劣，不是體驗單方面完勝物質。
- **來源機構**：Social Psychological and Personality Science
- **網址**：https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/1948550615619761；DOI: 10.1177/1948550615619761
- **以…為準**：WebFetch 取得的摘要（含直接引號的關鍵句，判斷為逐字或接近逐字）
- **信心等級**：likely（摘要頁面內容經過小模型轉述，未能 100% 確認是否為完整逐字摘要，建議正式引用前再核對一次原文 PDF）
- **原文引述**（摘要中直接以引號標示的句子）：
  > "Material purchases provide more frequent momentary happiness over time, whereas experiential purchases provide more intense momentary happiness on individual occasions."
- **完整引用**：Weidman, A. C., & Dunn, E. W. (2016). The unsung benefits of material things: material purchases provide more frequent momentary happiness than experiential purchases. *Social Psychological and Personality Science*, 7(4), 390–399.
- **備註**：這篇常被引用來說「體驗 vs. 物質」的結論沒有 Van Boven & Gilovich (2003) 講的那麼一刀切；後續 Kumar, Killingsworth & Gilovich 等人也提醒讀者不要因此誤解成「應該多買東西少買體驗」，因為長期滿意度、社會比較等面向上體驗仍佔優勢——這是文獻内部的持續辯論，不是單純的反例推翻。

### 4b. 回顧文獻：Gilovich, Kumar & Jampol (2015)
