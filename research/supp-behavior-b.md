## 9. Kuhnen & Knutson (2005)；Knutson & Bossaerts (2007) —— 大腦與冒險/迴避

### Kuhnen, C. M., & Knutson, B. (2005). The Neural Basis of Financial Risk Taking. *Neuron*, 47(5), 763–770.
DOI: 10.1016/j.neuron.2005.08.008

**狀態：verified**（直接下載並讀取作者 Stanford 個人網站掛的原文 PDF：`https://stanford.edu/~knutson/bad/kuhnen05.pdf`，抓到 Summary 全文）

原句（Summary）：

> "Nucleus accumbens activation preceded risky choices as well as risk-seeking mistakes, while anterior insula activation preceded riskless choices as well as risk-aversion mistakes."

主要結論：伏隔核（nucleus accumbens，獎賞／興奮迴路）活化，發生在冒險選擇與「冒過頭」的錯誤之前；前腦島（anterior insula，厭惡／焦慮迴路）活化，發生在迴避風險的選擇與「太保守」的錯誤之前——兩條不同的神經迴路分別把人推向冒險與推向迴避。

### Knutson, B., & Bossaerts, P. (2007). Neural Antecedents of Financial Decisions. *Journal of Neuroscience*, 27(31), 8174–8177.
DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1564-07.2007

**狀態：verified**（打開期刊原頁 `https://www.jneurosci.org/content/27/31/8174` 讀到摘要）

原句（摘要）：

> "...the ventral striatum plays a role in representation of expected reward, [while] the insula may play a more prominent role in the representation of expected risk."

主要結論：與 Kuhnen & Knutson 呼應——腹側紋狀體（ventral striatum，涵蓋伏隔核）活化預示「冒險」的財務決策；腦島活化預示「保守／迴避風險」的決策。兩篇合起來的意思：人在下財務決策前，這兩個腦區的活化程度就已經能預測他會偏向衝還是偏向縮。

Tversky, A., & Kahneman, D. (1992). Advances in Prospect Theory: Cumulative Representation of Uncertainty. *Journal of Risk and Uncertainty*, 5(4), 297–323.
DOI: **10.1007/BF00122574**

**狀態：verified**（下載開放鏡像 PDF `https://cemi.ehess.fr/docannexe/file/2780/tversjy_kahneman_advances.pdf`，用程式抽取全文，在第 311 頁找到原句）

## 11. Benartzi & Thaler (1995) —— 越常檢視、越不願持股

Benartzi, S., & Thaler, R. H. (1995). Myopic Loss Aversion and the Equity Premium Puzzle. *Quarterly Journal of Economics*, 110(1), 73–92.

**狀態：verified**（下載 NBER Working Paper 版本全文 PDF：`https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w4369/w4369.pdf`——此為同一篇論文 1993 年的 NBER 工作論文版，內容與 1995 年 QJE 正式版一致，抽取全文核對）

核心結論原句：

> "...when decision makers are loss averse, they will be more willing to take risks if they evaluate their performance (or have their performance evaluated) infrequently... two factors contribute to an investor being unwilling to bear the risks associated with holding equities, loss aversion and a short evaluation period. We refer to this combination as myopic loss aversion."

白話：損失趨避＋短評估週期＝「短視損失趨避」。检查投資組合的頻率越高，越容易看到（帳面）虧損、越痛，因此越不願意持有波動較大的股票資產；反過來，越少看盤、評估週期越長，越能忍受股票的波動而長期持有。

補充（摘要句）：模擬結果顯示，若投資人以「約一年」為評估週期，用展望理論已估計出的參數，正好可以解釋歷史上的股票溢酬（equity premium）之大小——"investors appear to choose portfolios as if they were operating with a time horizon of about one year."

## 12. Samuelson & Zeckhauser (1988)；Madrian & Shea (2001) —— 現狀偏誤與自動加入

### Samuelson, W., & Zeckhauser, R. (1988). Status Quo Bias in Decision Making. *Journal of Risk and Uncertainty*, 1(1), 7–59.

**狀態：verified**（下載原文 PDF：`https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/biases/1_J_Risk_Uncertainty_7_(Samuelson).pdf`，讀到摘要與定義句）

定義句（摘要）：

> "Most real decisions, unlike those of economics texts, have a status quo alternative—that is, doing nothing or maintaining one's current or previous decision. A series of decision-making experiments shows that individuals disproportionately stick with the status quo."

### Madrian, B. C., & Shea, D. F. (2001). The Power of Suggestion: Inertia in 401(k) Participation and Savings Behavior. *Quarterly Journal of Economics*, 116(4), 1149–1187.

**狀態：verified**（下載 NBER Working Paper 版本全文 PDF：`https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w7682/w7682.pdf`，內容與 QJE 正式版一致，抽取全文核對到具體數字）

自動加入利用惰性的證據（原文數字）：

> "The overall 401(k) participation rate in the study company prior to the adoption of automatic enrollment was 61%... approximately 86% of employees hired under automatic enrollment participate in the 401(k) plan."
>
> "The 401(k) participation rate of the WINDOW cohort at 3-15 months of tenure was 37%. This is less than half the 86% participation rate of the NEW cohort with a similar amount of tenure."

白話：同一家公司、同樣的方案內容，只是把「預設不加入、要自己申請」改成「預設加入、要自己申請退出」——參與率就從 61% 跳到 86%（在相同任職年資下比較，是 37% 對 86%，超過兩倍）。經濟條件完全沒變，光是「預設選項」（利用惰性/現狀偏誤）就讓行為天差地遠——這正是 Samuelson & Zeckhauser 現狀偏誤在真實世界的印證。

## 14. 相對剝奪感提高冒險行為

Haisley, E., Mostafa, R., & Loewenstein, G. (2008). Subjective relative income and lottery ticket purchases. *Journal of Behavioral Decision Making*, 21(3), 283–295.
DOI: 10.1002/bdm.588

**狀態：verified**（透過作者 Loewenstein 自己在 CMU 系上掛的全文 PDF：`https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/sds/docs/loewenstein/SubRelIncomeLottery.pdf`，讀到完整摘要）

主要結論（摘要原句）：

> "In two experiments conducted with low-income participants, we examine how implicit comparisons with other income classes increase low-income individuals' desire to play the lottery. In Experiment 1, participants were more likely to purchase lottery tickets when they were primed to perceive that their own income was low relative to an implicit standard."

白話：讓低收入受試者「感覺」自己比別人窮（相對剝奪感被觸發），會讓他們更想買彩券——也就是更願意賭一把翻身。這篇是關於彩券，「相對剝奪感→更願意冒險/賭」的邏輯與投資語境類推適用，但原研究對象是彩券而非投資組合，專篇引用時建議註明「原研究為彩券消費，非股票投資」以免誤導成直接的投資實證。

（Callan, Ellard, Shead & Hodgins (2008) 未進一步查證——上面 Haisley et al. 已足夠支撐論點且直接找到原文，故未使用剩餘額度重複查第二篇。）

## 15. 事後之明；近因偏誤

### Fischhoff, B. (1975). Hindsight ≠ Foresight: The Effect of Outcome Knowledge on Judgment Under Uncertainty. *Journal of Experimental Psychology: Human Perception and Performance*, 1(3), 288–299.
DOI: 10.1037/0096-1523.1.3.288

**狀態：verified**（下載原文 PDF：`https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Fischhoff_1975_Hindsight_is_not_equal_to_foresight.pdf`，讀到摘要全文）

原句（摘要）：

> "In Experiment 1, receipt of such outcome knowledge was found to increase the postdicted likelihood of reported events and change the perceived relevance of event-descriptive data, regardless of the likelihood of the outcome and the truth of the report. Judges were, however, largely unaware of the effect that outcome knowledge had on their perceptions."

白話：一旦知道結果，人會覺得「這個結果本來就比較可能發生」，還會不自覺地重新解讀事前的線索、覺得早就看得出來（也就是後來被稱為「creeping determinism 悄悄的決定論」的現象）；更關鍵的是，人完全沒意識到自己被「已知結果」污染了判斷。

### Greenwood, R., & Shleifer, A. (2014). Expectations of Returns and Expected Returns. *Review of Financial Studies*, 27(3), 714–746.

**狀態：verified**（下載 NBER Working Paper 版本全文 PDF：`https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w18686/w18686.pdf`，內容與 RFS 正式版一致，讀到摘要）

原句（摘要）：

> "We analyze time-series of investor expectations of future stock market returns from six data sources between 1963 and 2011. The six measures of expectations are highly positively correlated with each other, as well as with past stock returns and with the level of the stock market. However, investor expectations are strongly negatively correlated with model-based expected returns."

白話：投資人「主觀預期的未來報酬」跟「最近的實際報酬」高度正相關——最近漲越多，投資人就越樂觀地預期未來還會繼續漲（近因偏誤／外推捷思）；但這種主觀預期,恰恰跟學術模型算出來的「客觀預期報酬」是負相關的——也就是說,投資人越樂觀的時候,往往正是模型認為未來報酬該打折扣的時候。

## 16. Kahneman & Riepe (1998) —— 過度自信與投資

Kahneman, D., & Riepe, M. W. (1998). Aspects of Investor Psychology. *Journal of Portfolio Management*, 24(4), 52–65.

**狀態：verified（經第三方逐字轉載頁面確認，非期刊官網本身）**

查證過程：`jpm.pm-research.com`（期刊官網）與 ProQuest 均需訂閱，WebFetch 未能取得內文；改用 `capitalideasonline.com` 刊出的「原文逐字節錄」（該站聲明為對本文的直接摘錄 excerpts，非其自撰摘要），下載該頁 PDF 並抽取全文，讀到與書中常見引用一致的段落。建議專篇引用前，若條件允許，另以圖書館資料庫比對正式排版頁碼（本查證僅能確認文字內容，頁碼引用「52-65」係採期刊官網公開的卷期頁碼 metadata，非從轉載頁面驗證的頁內位置）。

原句（過度自信結論段）：

> "Unfortunately, few people are well-calibrated. A vast amount of research documents a highly systematic bias in subjective confidence intervals; there are far too many surprises, indicating that the intervals were set too tightly... This phenomenon, known as overconfidence, is widespread and robust. Overconfidence has been confirmed even when it is in the best interest of the research subjects to be well-calibrated."

以及：

> "Two groups of professionals have been found to be reasonably well-calibrated: meteorologists and handicappers at race tracks... When these conditions are not satisfied, overconfidence should be expected, for both experts and non-experts."

白話結論：絕大多數人（包括專業人士）對自己判斷的信心，系統性地高過其判斷實際的準確度——這就是過度自信偏誤，而且它很頑固：連受試者明明知道校準得好對自己更有利，過度自信依然存在。只有天氣預報員、賽馬賠率員這種「每天面對相似問題、做明確機率預測、又能很快拿到準確回饋」的職業,才比較不會過度自信——而大多數投資決策完全不具備這三個條件。
